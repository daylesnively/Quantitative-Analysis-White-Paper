# A Retroactive Evaluation of a Games-Based Health Education Curriculum

**[Read the full paper (PDF)](GRHWP(1).pdf)**

Grassroots Health is a games-based health education nonprofit, serving public middle schoolers in Washington, DC. Through a retroactive analysis of pre- and post-intervention survey data from the 2025-2026 school year, statistically significant score improvement metrics were found across all three curriculum domains.
